Terms of Service

The rules for using Scenario OmniChannel, on the web and in the mobile app.

At a glance

  • Scenario OmniChannel is a business support tool used by authorized employees of a business ("your Organization") to manage that Organization's customer conversations across Meta platforms (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Messenger).
  • The Scenario mobile app authenticates employees against the same backend as the web app, using session-cookie authentication — there is no separate, weaker mobile login.
  • We do not sell personal information or customer conversation data.
  • The mobile app contains no advertising SDK, no third-party analytics SDK, and no third-party crash-reporting SDK.
