User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: August 5, 2026 Contact: Mohamed.gad0487@gmail.com

This page outlines procedures for requesting removal of data connected to Scenario's OmniChannel application and associated Meta services — including data connected to the Scenario mobile app.

How to Request Deletion

Users should send correspondence to Mohamed.gad0487@gmail.com with the subject line “Scenario Data Deletion Request” and provide identifying details such as name, associated email address, connected social accounts or business numbers, and specifics regarding what information should be removed.

Mobile App If your request concerns a Scenario mobile app employee account, please also include your Scenario sign-in email and the name of the business (Organization) the account belongs to, or ask that Organization's administrator to submit the request on your behalf, since employee accounts are created and managed by the Organization.

Email a deletion request

What Happens Next

The company may verify your authorization before proceeding with deletion or anonymization of qualifying information. Some data may be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal, accounting, or regulatory compliance purposes.

Scenario Mobile App Employee Accounts Mobile App

When a deletion request concerns an employee account used with the Scenario mobile app, the following applies:

  • The employee account is deactivated and no longer able to sign in or be signed in to the mobile app.
  • The account record is retained by the Organization rather than immediately and permanently erased — the same way many business systems deactivate rather than hard-delete a former employee's account, so that the Organization's own conversation and audit history stays intact and attributable. It is not presented as, or intended to be, immediate permanent erasure.
  • The device's push-notification registration for that employee is removed, so the device stops receiving Scenario notifications.
  • Locally stored app data (session, cached email, on-device preferences) is removed by signing out of the app, or by deleting the app from the device.
  • Customer conversations and records the employee worked on belong to the Organization as its own business records and are handled under the Organization's own retention needs, not deleted merely because one employee's account is deactivated.

If a request requires information beyond deactivation — for example, erasing specific personal data fields, subject to the retention exceptions above — describe that explicitly in your email so it can be handled accordingly.

Disconnecting Meta Integrations

Users can revoke Scenario's access through Facebook/Meta business settings directly. However, disconnecting does not automatically delete previously processed or retained information. The email procedure should be used to request deletion of historical data.

Expected Response

Valid deletion requests will receive acknowledgment and processing within a timeframe aligned with applicable regulations and platform requirements.

For additional inquiries, contact Mohamed.gad0487@gmail.com.
